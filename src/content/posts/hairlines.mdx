## Why hairlines

A one-pixel rule carries the same information as a box shadow and costs nothing
in visual noise. On a drawing, rules divide; they do not decorate.

The remaining note to work out: how the rules should behave at high pixel
densities, where "one pixel" stops meaning one physical pixel.
